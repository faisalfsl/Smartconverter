Privacy Policy - Smart Converter

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

Smart Converter (package name: com.smartconverter.Smartconverter) respects the privacy of its users.

Information We Collect

Smart Converter does not directly collect, store, or maintain personal information from users. The app does not require account registration and does not ask users to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

All unit conversion calculations and user-entered conversion values are processed locally on the user's device and are not sent to the developer's servers.

Advertising

Smart Converter uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect or process certain information necessary to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services. This may include:

  • Device information and identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • App interactions
  • Advertising and performance information
  • Diagnostic information

The developer does not directly collect or maintain this information on its own servers. Information collected or processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

Smart Converter does not sell users' personal information. The app uses Google AdMob as a third-party advertising service, so certain information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners in accordance with their applicable privacy policies.

Permissions

Smart Converter only uses permissions required for the operation of the application and integrated advertising services.

The app does not access users' contacts, messages, call history, microphone, camera, photos, or precise location for its unit conversion functionality.

Data Retention and Deletion

Smart Converter does not provide user accounts and does not directly store personal user information on developer-controlled servers.

Any information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's own data retention and deletion policies.

Children's Privacy

Smart Converter does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party advertising services used by the app may process information according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

Security

Smart Converter does not maintain a developer-controlled database containing users' personal information. Third-party services integrated into the application are responsible for protecting information they process according to their own security and privacy practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, particularly if features, advertising services, or privacy practices within Smart Converter change.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the Smart Converter Google Play Store listing.
